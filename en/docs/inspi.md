# Sumerian Inspiration

The **Zigurate**'s syntax use names and concepts that come from *Sumerian* and *Acadian* language (Assyria and Babylonian)

The name "*Zigurate*" (derive directly of brazilian portuguese pronounce) is an inspiration by one of the most talked-about architectural creations of antiquity of story, the *Zigurate of Ur*, located in the province of *Dhi Qar* in Iraq.

> "The sumerian's existences fascinates me so much, the architecture, culture, language and your ideas and builds, which, for that time, seemed far too advanced."

### Keywords

- *uda*
- *lunig*
- *anki*
- *ak*
- *silim*
- *kud*
- *gaz*
- *gish*
- *dumu*
- *suma* (*Sumer*)

### Meanings

- *uda* = "Sun, time or day"
- *lunig* = "Man or cattle herder"
- *anki* = "Universe"
- *ak* = "Verb to do, act or build"
- *silim* = "Hello, grettings"
- *kud* = "Cut, broken"
- *gaz* = "Smash, kill"
- *gish* = "Tree"
- *dumu* = "Son, daughter or kid"
- *suma* = "Verb to give"
